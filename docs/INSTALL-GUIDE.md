# WeeklyClass LMS — how to install it on your phone

Share this with students and teachers. It takes about thirty seconds.

**The app lives at:** https://weeklyclass-lms.web.app

There is nothing to download from an app store. It is a web app that installs
itself, so it works on any phone, tablet or computer and updates on its own.

---

## Android (Chrome)

1. Open **https://weeklyclass-lms.web.app** in Chrome.
2. Tap the **⋮** menu at the top right.
3. Tap **Add to Home screen** (some phones say *Install app*).
4. Tap **Install**.

The WeeklyClass icon appears with your other apps.

## iPhone and iPad (Safari)

Safari does not offer an install button — it has to be done from the Share
menu, so this step is worth reading carefully.

1. Open **https://weeklyclass-lms.web.app** in **Safari**.
   Chrome on iPhone cannot install it; it must be Safari.
2. Tap the **Share** button (the square with an arrow, at the bottom).
3. Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**.
4. Tap **Add**.

## Computer (Chrome or Edge)

1. Open **https://weeklyclass-lms.web.app**.
2. Click the **install icon** in the address bar (a small screen with an arrow),
   or open the **⋮** menu and choose **Install**.

---

## After installing

- **Sign in** with your mobile number or username and your password.
- If you do not have an account yet, tap **Register** on the first screen.
- Forgotten your password? Tap **Forgot password?** and an administrator will
  send you a reset link.

## It works without internet

Once you have opened a page, it stays available offline — lessons you have
read, the Qur'an, prayer times, your reading plan. Anything you write while
offline is saved on your phone and sent automatically the moment you are back
online. A strip at the top of the screen tells you which state you are in.

## Choosing your language

The app is in **English, தமிழ், සිංහල and العربية**. Pick yours on the first
screen, or change it any time from the language button at the top of your
dashboard.
